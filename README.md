## 📖 About the Game

Orbit Legacy is a 3D offline management and spaceflight game for Android. You inherit your father's aerospace company at its lowest point: the funds are almost gone, the hangar is quiet, and the future depends on you.

Walk around the company campus, manage the finances, run missions from the control room, and keep every satellite in orbit. There is no internet requirement and no account to create. Open the app and play.

---

## 🌌 The Story

Every space company begins with a dream. This one began in a small hangar.

Your father spent his life on **Project Legacy**, an effort to build something that would outlast him. When he is gone, all that remains is the company, an empty campus, a shrinking bank balance, and a set of handwritten notes. One line stands out:

> *"Don't let the dream end in this hangar."*

You take the chair of the CEO. From here you decide how the company survives: which missions to take, how to spend every last credit, and who to trust with the work. Engineering, industry and spaceflight all meet on one campus, and the legacy is yours to build or lose.

## ✨ Features

- 🌍 **3D open campus**: explore the facilities on foot with a virtual joystick and switchable camera views
- 🏢 **Company management**: track funds, date and weather through the company terminal
- 🛰️ **Mission control**: monitor missions, handle emergency alarms and operate the crane by hand
- 📱 **Boss phone**: chat with your manager and other contacts
- 🎥 **Security system**: switch between security cameras and use the CEO's PC
- 🚗 **Vehicles**: get in and out of vehicles around the campus
- 📸 **Photo mode**: freeze the action and frame the perfect shot
- 💾 **Save system**: continue your progress anytime
- 📴 **Fully offline**: no connection needed

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Walk | Left virtual joystick |
| Look around | Drag on the right side of the screen |
| Interact | **Use** button |
| Enter / exit vehicle | Vehicle button |
| Switch camera | Camera button |
| Zoom camera | **+** / **−** buttons |
| Crane control | On-screen crane pad |

> 💡 Best played in **landscape** mode. If your phone is upright, the game will ask you to rotate it.

## 📲 Installation Guide

1. **Download** the APK with the button at the top (or from the [Releases](https://github.com/Abid-developerApK/orbit-legacy-android-game/releases) page)
2. **Open** the downloaded `OrbitLegacy-Android.apk` from your notification bar or file manager
3. If Android asks, **allow installing from unknown sources** for your browser or file manager
4. Tap **Install** and wait a few seconds
5. **Open** Orbit Legacy, rotate your phone to landscape, and tap **New Journey**

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| "App not installed" | Uninstall any older copy first, and make sure you have free storage |
| Install is blocked | Settings → allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager |
| Play Protect warning | Tap **More details** → **Install anyway** (the app is distributed outside the Play Store) |
| Screen looks cramped | Rotate the phone to landscape |
| Progress missing | Use **Continue** on the main menu to load your save |
